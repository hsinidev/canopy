Game Overview


What is Canopy?

Canopy is a browser-based game where players control a bird as it flies through a network of interconnected canopies, collecting items and avoiding obstacles. The objective is to collect the highest number of items while navigating the ever-changing environment.



To play Canopy, you will use the following controls:
- Use the mouse to move your bird up, down, left, or right.
- Press Ctrl+Alt+Arrow keys (or Command+Option+Arrows on a Mac) to rotate your bird 90 degrees clockwise or counter-clockwise.
- Use the Tab key to switch between different canopies.

The objective is to collect items such as coins, stars, and power-ups while navigating through the challenging terrain. The game features a variety of obstacles, including treacherous ravines, spinning blades, and animatronic enemies.


Tips and Tricks for Success

Here are some tips to help you succeed in Canopy:

- Start with easy levels and gradually move on to harder ones as your skills improve.
- Use the spin attack to navigate through tight spaces and avoid obstacles.
- Collect coins strategically, using them to upgrade your bird's speed or agility.
- Don't be afraid to sacrifice a few items for better opportunities later in the game.
